## Section 4 — Expected Results & Risks *(initial)*

I understand and accept that:

- **Results vary** by person, starting shade, enamel condition, and lifestyle
  (coffee, tea, wine, tobacco). Typical improvement is 4–10 shades; no
  specific result is guaranteed.
- **Restorations do not whiten.** Crowns, veneers, bonding, and fillings will
  not change shade, which may result in uneven color against natural teeth.
- **Temporary sensitivity** of the teeth or gums can occur during or after
  the session and usually resolves within 24–48 hours.
- **Gum irritation** (temporary white spots or tenderness) can occur if gel
  contacts the gums and typically resolves within hours.
- For best results, I should avoid staining foods and drinks for 48 hours
  after the session ("white diet") per the aftercare guidance provided.
